Cross-platform Kotlin Multiplatform starter scaffold for Android and iOS.
The Compose UI and learning logic are shared; child name and age persist locally and can be edited from the learning home screen.
Activities include memory match, age-banded maths, word and number puzzles, patterns, generated solvable mazes, lane-dodging car driving, an offline pack of eight public-domain line-art coloring pages, general knowledge and colour quizzes, full-screen finger drawing, and shuffled offline learning-buddy facts. Coloring fills persist per page; taps fill enclosed regions in the selected image, and children can change pages at any time. Completed quizzes, memory games, mazes, and driving challenges advance through numbered stages. Quiz and buddy prompts have animated emoji illustrations; incorrect quiz feedback does not reveal the right answer. Profile setup has a full-screen animated sky with a vector-style plane, blinking stars, flying birds, fish, and a dinosaur.
The learning home now has an animated landscape banner and original vector illustrations for each activity tile; every game screen opens with a theme-matched illustrated scene. Memory cards, maze walls and explorer, and the driving game’s car and road cones use original vector artwork rather than emoji-only game pieces. The drawing canvas fills the screen with floating controls, and coloring selectors preview the bundled offline image pack. Profile name entry plays a brief typing tone and animates a personalized greeting. Android supports independently muted sound effects and soft generated background music, controlled from the gear icon on the learning home screen. Selecting a game produces a brief card pop/glow and toy-like click. Child profiles, audio settings, and completed stages are stored locally; age determines age-banded question sets and difficulty. iOS stores profile/settings/progress, but audio playback is not implemented.
Not yet device-tested. iOS builds require macOS and Xcode. Local AI runtime, production RAG index, and comprehensive UI/integration tests remain to be implemented.
